# Business Problem

## Scenario

Management wants to understand customer purchasing behavior and determine
whether the business is successfully converting first-time buyers into
repeat customers.

They want to understand:

- How large and valuable the customer base is
- How many customers return after their first purchase
- How retention changes over time
- Which customer segments generate repeat revenue
- Which factors are associated with repeat purchasing
- How repeat purchasing affects overall revenue

## Analytical Objective

The objective of this analysis is to investigate customer retention and
revenue behavior using SQL and determine whether meaningful patterns exist
within the available data.

The analysis does not assume that customer retention is improving or
declining. Findings will be based on the data.

## Key Questions

1. What does the customer base look like?
2. Are customers coming back?
3. What does retention look like by cohort?
4. Who or what is associated with repeat purchasing?
5. What is the revenue impact of repeat customers?
